Signpost

A journey-blind capability resolver. Providers declare their own capabilities at their own origins; Signpost fetches those declarations and answers exactly one question — “where can capability X be performed?” — by transparent lexical retrieval over the providers’ own words. It holds no objective, session, cursor, sequence, history, or “next”. The generic agent owns the whole journey.
